Blackjack Streak

Privacy policy

Blackjack Streak has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad service uses to show ads.

Last updated November 17, 2026. This policy covers the Blackjack Streak app for iPhone and this website, made by Thaer Kort ("I", "me").

What stays on your phone

Your scores, stats, tokens, unlocked items, pass and challenge progress, and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. The app also keeps a short list of recent error messages on your phone so a bug report can include them. I can't see any of this. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send it to me.

Reports you send

If you tap Report a problem, the app opens an email in your own mail app with your phone model, iOS version, app version and recent error messages filled in. You can read and edit it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. I use these emails only to fix problems and reply to you.

Game Center leaderboard

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is sent to Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboard with your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; I only see the public leaderboard. To stop, sign out in iPhone Settings › Game Center.

Ads and the data they use

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, the only third-party software in the app (Game Center is part of iOS). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks)
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system version and language
  • An approximate location based on your IP address
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Crash and performance information about the ads

Google uses this data as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the first time you open the game, iPhone asks whether the app may track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads still appear but are less personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Europe and the UK: you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your answer in the game under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Deleting your data: Settings › Reset all progress deletes everything the game has stored on your phone. Deleting the app does the same. Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing else to delete. To ask Google about ad data, use the link above.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address to deliver the pages.

Children

Blackjack Streak is rated 18+ because it simulates a casino card game. It is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

What I don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or in-app purchases
  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No real-money gambling, prizes or payouts

Changes and contact
