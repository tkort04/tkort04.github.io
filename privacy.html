Privacy policy

Blackjack Streak has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad service uses to show ads.

Last updated November 7, 2026. This policy covers the Blackjack Streak app for iPhone, made by Thaer Kort ("I", "me").

What stays on your phone

Your score, best score, win/loss record, remaining chips and settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. I can't see them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Ads and the data they use

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the AdMob software in the app may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks)
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system version and language
  • An approximate location based on your IP address
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Crash and performance information about the ads

Google uses this data as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the first time you open the game, iPhone asks whether the app may track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads still appear but are less personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Europe and the UK: you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your answer from the game's ? screen under Privacy choices.

Children

Blackjack Streak is rated 18+ because it simulates a casino card game. It is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

What I don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or in-app purchases
  • No selling of your data
  • No real-money gambling, prizes or payouts

Changes and contact
