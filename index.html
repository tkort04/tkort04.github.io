21 Streak

A card game about one number. Get closer to 21 than the CPU without going over. Every winning hand is a point, and the only question is how far you can push your score before your hearts run out.

How it works

  • Win a hand, score a point. Hit 21 for two points and a heart back. A tie changes nothing.
  • Five hearts. A loss costs one. Two wins in a row earn one back.
  • Power-ups on the left of the table: a peek at your next card every 3 points, a rescue to redeal a bad hand each time you drop to your last heart, and an Instant 10 for every 21.
  • Practice mode checks every move against basic strategy, with a deck tracker to train your memory and no score at stake.
  • The pass and challenges unlock backgrounds, card backs and music as you play. Tokens are earned in the game only and can't be bought.

21 Streak is for entertainment only. There is no betting of any kind, nothing to win, and nothing to buy.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? In the game, open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Report a problem. It opens an email with your phone model and app version already filled in. You can also write to bdeyes12@gmail.com. I read every message.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. The game keeps a second copy in the app's own storage, so it's restored if iOS clears the first one. Deleting the app deletes both.

Start over or delete your data: Settings › Reset all progress. This removes everything the game has stored.

Ad privacy choices: Settings › Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
