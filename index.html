Blackjack Streak

A blackjack game with no money in it. Every winning hand is a point, and the only question is how far you can push your score before your chips run out.

How it works

  • Win a hand, score a point. A tie changes nothing.
  • Five chips. A loss costs one. Two wins in a row earn one back.
  • Peek at your next card every 3 wins (save them up), split pairs into two hands, and use a rescue on your last chip.

Blackjack Streak is for entertainment only. There is no real-money gambling, nothing to win, and nothing to buy.
